# lame.min.js

`lame.min.js` is the unmodified browser build of [lamejs 1.2.1](https://github.com/zhuker/lamejs) (a JavaScript port of the LAME MP3 encoder),
licensed under the GNU LGPL v3 (https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.html). It is used by the Research page to save the merged Quran audio as an MP3 file.
